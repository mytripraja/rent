# V8.32 — Property-Scoped Tenant Notifications

- Enterprise resident-facing notifications no longer download every tenant user account.
- Tenant accounts are queried by the relevant house IDs in Firestore-safe batches of 30.
- The tenant role is still checked locally to avoid a composite-index requirement.
- Existing communication, parcel, and visitor notification workflows are preserved.
- No tenant, house, payment, or historical data is deleted.
