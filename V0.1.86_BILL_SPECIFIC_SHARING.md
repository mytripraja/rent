# v0.1.86 — Bill-specific sharing and tenant payment receipts

- Water and EB bill sharing now generates a bill-specific tenant portal URL with bill type and bill document ID.
- The selected bill link is included in WhatsApp, email, other share options, and can be copied directly.
- Unauthenticated users are redirected through sign-in and returned to the bill-specific tenant route.
- The tenant bill list filters to the linked bill, while the data query remains restricted to the signed-in tenant house. Query parameters do not grant access to another house.
- Water bill payment now offers a configured UPI deep link with the resident share amount prefilled.
- Water and EB payment submission show a reference ID receipt that can be screenshotted; payments remain pending owner verification.

Note: UPI app launch depends on the device and installed apps. A UPI deep link cannot guarantee payment success or automatically verify the transaction.
