# Rental Manager V6.6 — House Assets, Wi-Fi Sharing & News

## New features
- House Assets & Inspection: per-house asset register, condition tracking, quantities, notes, and move-in/routine/vacate/repair inspections.
- Community Wi-Fi Sharing: owner and tenant can offer an internet-sharing listing tied to their house, with speed, monthly cost, capacity, notes, optional contact number, interest requests, and pause/resume/delete controls.
- Wi-Fi listings never store Wi-Fi passwords. Users should follow their ISP terms; commercial/public resale may require separate authorization.
- News Hub: two categories in the UI (Local and India/Common). The backend reads approved RSS feeds server-side and only exposes short headline metadata + original links.
- PIB India is included as a common-feed default. Local publisher feeds are configured through NEWS_FEED_URLS.

## Local news configuration
Set a Vercel Production environment variable:

NEWS_FEED_URLS=https://approved-local-source.example/rss.xml,https://another-approved-source.example/feed.xml

The application labels these feeds as Local. Only add feeds whose publisher terms permit your intended use. Do not copy full articles into Firestore or the application.

## Wi-Fi sharing note
The app is a neighbour-to-neighbour coordination board. It does not store passwords or operate an ISP/reseller service. Payment tracking can be added later, but the current version records the proposed monthly share and contact/interest workflow.

## Security
- House assets/inspections are owner-only.
- Wi-Fi listing creation is limited to a signed-in owner or tenant and is tied to the caller's own house for tenants.
- Wi-Fi interest creation is tied to the signed-in seeker.


### Place-specific local feeds
For different places, use `NEWS_LOCAL_FEEDS_JSON` as a JSON map. Example:

`NEWS_LOCAL_FEEDS_JSON={"Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu":["https://approved-source.example/coimbatore/rss.xml"],"Salem, Tamil Nadu":["https://approved-source.example/salem/rss.xml"]}`

The UI's place selector then selects the matching local feed set. `NEWS_FEED_URLS` remains a general local-feed fallback.
