# V8.91 — Rent submission validation

- Validates house and tenant IDs, month format, positive bounded amount, payment date, and supported payment method before any proof upload.
- Requires a collector house for neighbor-collected rent and a receiver for cash entries.
- Writes normalized values to the payment document.
- Prevents malformed client submissions from leaving private proof uploads without a corresponding payment document.

This is client-side validation only. It does not replace Firestore security rules or server-side authorization, and it does not enforce one payment per house/month because split payments and other workflows may be legitimate.
