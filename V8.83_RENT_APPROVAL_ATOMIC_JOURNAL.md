# V8.83 — Atomic rent approval accounting

- Rent approval now commits the payment status and its balanced Cash / Bank and Rental Income journal lines in one Firestore transaction.
- The transaction rechecks that the payment is still waiting for approval and validates its house/property and amount.
- Deterministic journal document IDs and payment-to-journal metadata prevent a retry from silently creating duplicate journal lines. Existing journal entries cause the approval to stop for manual review.
- Notifications and activity logging occur after the transaction commits. These side effects are not part of the Firestore transaction and may need retry if they fail.

## Validation

Syntax and archive integrity checks only. No live Firestore, concurrency, or production build test was performed.
